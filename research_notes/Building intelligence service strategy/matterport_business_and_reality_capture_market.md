# Matterport and the 3D Interior Capture / Digital Twin Market as a Business (as of 2026-11-13)

Scope: who buys interior 3D capture and digital twins, what they use them for, what they pay, their pains, Matterport's trajectory under CoStar (Feb 2025 to Nov 2026), and competitors and market size. Research done 2026-11-13. Most Matterport-specific numbers since the acquisition come from CoStar earnings calls (transcripts on Fool.com / VectorShift) and SEC filings. CoStar does not report Matterport as its own segment, so standalone 2026 Matterport revenue is not public.

---

## 1. Matterport customer segments, use cases, scale metrics and pricing

### Takeaway
Matterport is a horizontal "digital twin" platform. It serves residential and commercial real estate, facilities management, retail, AEC, insurance/restoration and travel/hospitality, with about 1.1–1.2M subscribers, about 14.1M spaces and 50.7B sq ft under management at end-2024. No single customer is large (the top 10 are under 10% of subscription revenue). Pricing moved in October 2026 from cheap self-serve tiers (Free/Starter/Professional/Business) to a space-count ladder on Professional ($90/mo for 10 spaces) plus Enterprise. This fits CoStar's stated shift away from low-end iPhone users toward professional and enterprise customers.

### Cited Findings
**Segments and use cases (FY2024 10-K, pre-acquisition, so older info):**
- The 10-K lists customer markets as residential and commercial real estate, facilities management, retail, AEC, insurance and repair, and travel and hospitality. Manufacturing and oil & gas are named as expansion targets. Government is not listed as a current core segment. — [Matterport FY2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819394/000181939425000007/mttr-20241231.htm)
- Matterport sells to "enterprises, from Fortune 100 companies to small and medium-sized businesses". "Many Fortune 1000" companies are subscribers, and "less than 10% of our total subscription revenue [is] generated from our top 10 subscribers" (a long-tail, low-concentration base). — [Matterport FY2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819394/000181939425000007/mttr-20241231.htm)
- The 10-K notes that small business subscriptions are seasonal (tied to the real estate cycle). — [Matterport FY2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819394/000181939425000007/mttr-20241231.htm)
- Capture devices: Matterport Pro3 and Pro2 cameras, third-party 360 cameras, Leica BLK360, and smartphone apps. Services include an "ADA Compliant Digital Twin" and an Enterprise Cloud Software Platform. The company operates in more than 170 countries, with sales teams in the US, Europe and Asia, and had 455 full-time employees at 12/31/2024. — [Matterport FY2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819394/000181939425000007/mttr-20241231.htm)
- Property Intelligence (launched **Feb 15, 2024**): AI-generated measurements, layouts, editing and reporting from the twin. It also added a CAD add-on (point cloud to DWG/DXF) and schematic floor plan customization. Targets are agents and marketers, design and construction teams, and facilities and property managers (retail chains, restaurants, factories). The release claims "nearly one billion virtual tours served each year." — [Matterport press release](https://matterport.com/news/matterport-launches-property-intelligence-transforming-real-estate)
- Insurance/restoration: a Verisk–Matterport integration lets adjusters and restorers order a **TruePlan** sketch (SKX files) of a Matterport model "with a single click in Xactimate". Partner mpartial auto-detects objects in the twin and generates Xactimate line-item estimates, each deep-linked to the twin. — [Verisk newsroom](https://www.verisk.com/company/newsroom/new-verisk-and-matterport-integration-accelerates-sketching-process-for-insurance-claims/); [Matterport mpartial case study](https://matterport.com/industries/case-studies/mpartial-creates-timely-accurate-repair-estimates-faster-and-fairer-claims). Note: both are vendor sources, and I found no independent data on cycle time or dispute reduction.
- AEC: Matterport markets LOD 200 "BIM-ready" IFC/RVT outputs and a free Revit plug-in (vendor claim). — [Matterport blog](https://matterport.com/blog/experts-for-experts-how-matterport-streamlines-bim-creation-and-as-built)
- Rental/multifamily marketing is now the largest proven use case inside CoStar. Apartments.com has **~250,000 Matterport 3D tours and 1,500+ 3D exteriors**. Renters spend "46% more time" on listings with Matterport, and these listings get "56 times more tour requests per listing" (Q1 2026 call). — [CoStar Q1 2026 call, Fool.com](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/28/costar-csgp-q1-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
- In Q3 2026, renters viewed "more than 65 million tours, up 99% year-over-year". Listings with Matterport "generated over 4x more leads and nearly 6x as many tours or application requests." — [CoStar Q3 2026 call, Fool.com](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/28/costar-csgp-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
- Domain (Australia, also CoStar-owned) launched Matterport in April 2026 and bundles it into premium listing packages to drive upgrades. — [CoStar Q1 2026 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/28/costar-csgp-q1-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

**Scale metrics (end-2024, last standalone disclosure):**
- About **14.1M spaces under management** at 12/31/2024, up from 11.7M (2023) and 9.2M (2022). About **50.7B sq ft** captured. (The 10-K's Genesis section cites "more than 38 billion square feet", an internal inconsistency.) — [Matterport FY2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819394/000181939425000007/mttr-20241231.htm). Note: the "~12M+ spaces" in the brief is a 2023-era figure.
- Subscribers: the 10-K says "over 1.1 million" at 12/31/2024, while the Q4 2024 earnings release says 1.2M (+23% YoY). The two sources differ slightly. Neither gives a paid-subscriber count. — [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819394/000181939425000007/mttr-20241231.htm); [Q4 2024 release via Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/31134237/matterport-announces-fourth-quarter-2024-financial-results-with-over-50-billion-in-square-feet-under-management-up-33-and-total-subscription-revenue-up-14-year-over-year)
- FY2024 revenue was **$169.7M** (+8%): subscription **$99.6M** (+14%, ~59%), services **$41.3M** (+10%, ~24%) and product (cameras) **$28.8M** (−12%, ~17%, due to lower Pro2/Pro3 volume). Net loss was ~$256.6M (2024) and ~$199.1M (2023), with an accumulated deficit of ~$1.035B. — [Matterport FY2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819394/000181939425000007/mttr-20241231.htm); [Q4 2024 release](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/31134237/matterport-announces-fourth-quarter-2024-financial-results-with-over-50-billion-in-square-feet-under-management-up-33-and-total-subscription-revenue-up-14-year-over-year)
- As of Q3 2026, subscription is "now 70% of its revenue" (up from ~59% in 2024). — [CoStar Q3 2026 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/28/costar-csgp-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

**Pricing (current and historical):**

Professional is now a **space ladder**, billed annually: 10 spaces $90/mo ($108 month-to-month; $1,080/yr), 20 spaces $125, 30 $165, 50 $245, 75 $345, 100 $430, 150 $585, and 200+ contact sales. Enterprise is "contact sales". - **New Pro3 Plus Package:** a Pro3 LiDAR camera plus Professional 50 plan, listed at $4,495 and marked down to **$1,995** (or $245/mo for 24 months). — [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/matterport/diffs/2026W42)
- **Prior tiers (now outdated):** Free $0 (1 active space, 2 users); Starter $12/mo annual ($14 monthly; 5–20 spaces, 3 users); Professional $58/mo annual ($69 monthly; 20 spaces); Business $296/mo annual ($355 monthly; 100–300 spaces, 50 users). — [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/matterport/diffs/2026W42). Older articles also quote Starter $9.99 and Business $309. — [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/matterport/pricing)
- One review site (frontdeskreview, "updated Nov 2026") still says paid plans "start at $14/mo (Starter)". This conflicts with the PricingSaaS capture and is likely stale. — [frontdeskreview](https://frontdeskreview.com/software/virtual-tour-software/matterport/)
- Add-ons such as MatterPak, schematic floor plans, BIM/E57/CAD files, TruePlan and Capture Services (on-demand technicians) exist, but I did not find current 2026 prices from a source I could open (see Gaps).
- CoStar strategy: Matterport is "embedded in subscription or advertising fees" across Apartments.com, LoopNet, Homes.com and Lands, so the advertiser pays for the tour indirectly. Pro3 hardware prices were cut while SaaS prices for regular users go up: a "razor-and-razor-blade strategy" (Florance, Q1 2026). — [CoStar Q1 2026 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/28/costar-csgp-q1-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

### Inferences
- The buyer base is very wide and shallow: about 1.1M+ subscribers on about $100M of 2024 subscription revenue, which is under $100 per subscriber per year on average. Most subscribers are free or very low-tier. Enterprise accounts (facilities, retail chains, AEC, insurers) are where the dollars per account are, and CoStar is chasing exactly that (see Section 2).
- Inside CoStar, the most monetized use case is **marketing**: rental and for-sale listing tours that lift lead volume, paid through listing ad packages. **Operational** uses (FM, AEC, insurance) are a smaller, enterprise-sales-driven business. A "building intelligence" service that serves operational buyers would address the less-penetrated part of the base.
- Removing Starter and Business and moving to an "active spaces" ladder makes the price explicitly per space under management. That is a natural unit to price a building-intelligence layer on top of (e.g., $/space/month or $/sq ft).

### Gaps
- No public 2025–2026 counts for subscribers, paid subscribers or spaces under management. CoStar stopped disclosing these after the acquisition.
- Enterprise vs. SMB revenue mix is not disclosed. The 10-K only says the top 10 are under 10% of subscription revenue.
- Current list prices for add-ons (MatterPak, BIM files, TruePlan, Capture Services per-scan fees) not verified from a fetchable official page.
- Government/public safety and travel use cases: no quantified data found beyond the 10-K segment list.

## 2. What happened to Matterport after the CoStar acquisition (Feb 2025 – Nov 2026)

### Takeaway
Since the deal closed on Feb 28, 2025 (~$1.6B), Matterport has run as a CoStar business unit. It contributed $147M of revenue in the 10 months of 2025, and its subscription growth sped up from high single digits to 16–19% in H1 2026 before easing to 11% in Q3 2026. CoStar uses it as a differentiator for Apartments.com, Homes.com, LoopNet and Domain, and as content for its AI assistants (Homes AI; Apartments.com Ai launched June 2026; LLM chat inside tours ~July 2026). On the standalone business, CoStar has cut camera prices, pushed SaaS, rebuilt the enterprise sales force, restarted hardware work (Matterport 4 planned for late 2027) and targets Matterport profitability "by the end of 2027".

### Cited Findings
**Deal and financial contribution:**
- The acquisition closed Feb 28, 2025. RJ Pittman stayed on as CEO of Matterport, now a business unit of CoStar. — [Matterport blog, 2/28/2025](https://matterport.com/blog/matterports-next-chapter-as-part-of-costar-group)
- The deal was valued at about $1.6B. — [GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/2875048/costar-csgp-finalizes-16b-matterport-acquisition-to-expand-aipowered-real-estate-platform); [Inman](https://news.inman.com/2025/02/27/costar-group-looks-to-hire-1k-employees-in-2025/) (Inman reported CoStar would "gain 500 employees from Matterport")
- Matterport revenue contribution: **$15.9M** (Feb 28–Mar 31, 2025), **$43.9M** in Q3 2025, **$103.8M** for 9M 2025, and **$147M** for FY2025 (as an acquisition-driven increase in "Other commercial real estate" revenue). — [CoStar FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1057352/000105735226000020/csgp-20251231.htm); [CoStar Q3 2025 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1057352/000105735225000120/csgp-20250930.htm); [CoStar Q1 2025 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1057352/000105735225000067/csgp-20250331.htm). ## 3. Competitive landscape: who wins which segment and why

### Takeaway
The market splits by accuracy requirement and buyer:
- **Listing marketing:** Matterport competes with Zillow 3D Home (free, built into Zillow Showcase), iGUIDE (measurement-grade floor plans, per-project pricing) and cheap 360-tour tools (Kuula, CloudPano, 3DVista, Ricoh360 Tours).
- **Construction progress:** OpenSpace, DroneDeploy/StructionSite and Cupix own 360 walk-through capture linked to BIM and schedules.
- **Survey-grade as-builts and scan-to-BIM:** NavVis, Leica (BLK360/BLK2GO/RTC), FARO and Trimble own mm-level work.
- **Low end:** iPhone LiDAR apps (Polycam and Apple RoomPlan-based apps) commoditize "good-enough" floor plans.

Matterport's edge is breadth, its installed library (14M+ spaces) and now CoStar distribution. Its weak points are survey-grade accuracy and construction-workflow depth.

### Cited Findings
**Residential and listing marketing:**
- Zillow provides Zillow 3D Home "to the industry for free" and syndicates 3D tours and interactive floor plans to Realtor.com. Zillow Showcase (3D tour, interactive floor plan, AI virtual staging added Sept 2025, SkyTour aerials) is on "nearly 10%" of new for-sale listings and is designed not to exceed 10% of listings in a market. — [Zillow Group news](https://www.zillowgroup.com/news/new-zillow-and-realtor-com-agreement-expands-access-to-interactive-listing-media); [Zillow Showcase facts](https://grow.zillow.com/showcase-facts); [Zillow AI staging release](https://zillow.mediaroom.com/2025-09-10-Zillow-brings-AI-powered-Virtual-Staging-to-Showcase-listings)
- 62% of buyers said they wish more listings had 3D tours (Zillow survey). — [Zillow Showcase facts](https://grow.zillow.com/showcase/showcase-facts-seller)
- iGUIDE: laser-equipped camera, ANSI Z765-compliant floor plans, per-project pricing with no monthly subscription, no dollhouse view. — [Travvir comparison](https://travvir.com/blog/en/top-matterport-alternatives); [Panoee](https://panoee.com/matterport-alternative) (vendor-affiliated comparison blogs)
- Low-cost tour tools: Kuula from about $20/mo, 3DVista one-time license about $499. **Survey-grade / AEC scanning:**

**Phone LiDAR:**
- Polycam's floor plans need a LiDAR iPhone plus the Polycam Business plan. Its pipeline starts from Apple's RoomPlan, which also powers many competing apps (RoomVector, FloorScan, etc.). — [Polycam blog](https://poly.cam/blog/how-to-create-floor-plans-using-your-iphone-in-10-minutes); [Polycam pipeline post](https://poly.cam/blog/how-we-turn-raw-spatial-data-into-a-floor-plan-you-can-build-from-inside-polycams-floor-plan-pipeline)

- Polycam markets itself as a Matterport alternative for small teams. — [Polycam blog](https://poly.cam/blog/best-matterport-alternatives-for-small-teams-creators-in-2026)

### Inferences
- **Residential listings:** Zillow's free 3D Home and Showcase is the structural threat in for-sale US listings. CoStar answers by bundling Matterport into Homes.com packages, so each portal ties a capture format to its own distribution.
- **Rentals/multifamily:** Matterport plus Apartments.com is the strongest position, with proven lead lift and scale (250K tours, 65M views per quarter).
- **Construction:** OpenSpace, DroneDeploy and Cupix win because they tie capture to schedule, BIM and progress tracking, and work at hardhat-walk speed. Matterport is mostly not the system of record there.
- **Survey/AEC as-builts:** NavVis, Leica, FARO and Trimble win on accuracy. Matterport's E57 import suggests it is positioning as a hosting and viewing layer for their point clouds rather than competing on hardware accuracy.
- **Insurance/restoration:** Matterport has a defensible niche through the Xactimate/TruePlan integration (Verisk is the industry standard).
- **Commodity end:** RoomPlan-based phone apps erode demand for simple floor plans and tours. This is consistent with CoStar's exit from the low-end tiers.
- No competitor clearly ties interior twins to authoritative property records, ownership and leasing data, and aerial/exterior imagery. That linkage is CoStar's unique asset combination.

### Gaps
- No market-share data for virtual tours, construction capture or laser scanning was found.
- No 2026 data was found on Ricoh360 Tours, CloudPano or Kuula traction, Cupix funding/ARR, or DroneDeploy financials.
- Most competitor comparisons come from vendor-affiliated blogs, so treat their price and feature claims as indicative only.

## 4. Buyer pain points

### Takeaway
Direct, independent evidence of buyer pain is thin; most sources are vendor marketing. The documented or inferable pains are:
- Ongoing per-space hosting costs and rising SaaS prices
- Accuracy too low for survey-grade or BIM-critical work, while phone capture is "sketch-grade"
- Format conversion (point cloud to CAD/BIM/Xactimate) sold as separate paid add-ons with turnaround delays
- Scans that capture one point in time and go stale
- Twins not linked to building records, exteriors or semantic (object/condition) data, though CoStar is now working on this

### Cited Findings
- **Cost and pricing pressure:** CoStar is explicitly raising SaaS prices for regular users. Starter and Business tiers (the cheapest multi-space and the mid-market plan) were removed in Oct 2026. — [CoStar Q1 2026 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/28/costar-csgp-q1-2026-earnings-call-transcript/); [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/matterport/diffs/2026W42)
- **Hosting lock-in:** tours are priced by "active spaces", and a third-party guide says "tours stop hosting if you stop paying" (about $20/mo per active space, unverified). — [Travvir pricing explainer](https://travvir.com/blog/en/matterport-pricing-explained) (unverified; vendor-competitor blog)
- **Accuracy limits:** Pro3 is about ±20 mm at 10 m vs 1–5 mm for survey LiDAR, so tolerance-critical work needs a second capture technology. — [Reality Capture Experts](https://realitycaptureexperts.com/matterport-accuracy/). One practitioner on a Pro2-era forum estimated about 2% variance ("fine for ordering paint, not for manufacturing") and suspected the software squares up walls that are slightly off 90 degrees (speculation). — [WGAN forum](https://www.wegetaroundnetwork.com/topic/16637/page/1/matterport-pro-2-accuracy/)
- **Phone capture accuracy:** iPhone LiDAR plans are "sketch-grade" (about 1 inch). — [illustrarch](https://illustrarch.com/?p=110334)
- **Format and conversion friction:** BIM (IFC/RVT, LOD 200), CAD (DWG/DXF) and Xactimate (TruePlan SKX) outputs are separate add-on deliverables. One capture provider quotes about 48-hour TruePlan turnaround. — [Matterport BIM blog](https://matterport.com/blog/experts-for-experts-how-matterport-streamlines-bim-creation-and-as-built); [Matterport Property Intelligence release](https://matterport.com/news/matterport-launches-property-intelligence-transforming-real-estate); [Verisk](https://www.verisk.com/company/newsroom/new-verisk-and-matterport-integration-accelerates-sketching-process-for-insurance-claims/)
- **Data silos / multi-source capture:** Matterport added E57 import in 2026 because enterprises hold point clouds from other scanners. — [VectorShift Q2 2026 transcript](https://vectorshift.ai/research/companies/costar-group/earnings/2026-q2)
- **Interior/exterior disconnect:** CoStar is building Gaussian-splat exteriors, "X-ray" exteriors and an outside-to-inside experience, which suggests the separation was a recognized gap. — [VectorShift Q2 2026](https://vectorshift.ai/research/companies/costar-group/earnings/2026-q2); [CoStar Q1 2026 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/28/costar-csgp-q1-2026-earnings-call-transcript/); [Apartments.com NAA 2026](https://www.apartments.com/grow/learning-center/naa-product-releases-2026)
- **Semantic understanding:** the main third-party example of object-level understanding is mpartial, which detects objects and maps them to Xactimate line items. Matterport's own Property Intelligence covers measurements and layouts, and the launch release does not mention object or condition detection. — [mpartial case study](https://matterport.com/industries/case-studies/mpartial-creates-timely-accurate-repair-estimates-faster-and-fairer-claims); [Property Intelligence release](https://matterport.com/news/matterport-launches-property-intelligence-transforming-real-estate)
- **Insurance disputes:** a restoration firm's testimonial says sharing models "reduced the number of inquiries and disputes from insurance adjusters." This is a vendor testimonial. — [Matterport insurance blog](https://matterport.com/blog/matterport-disrupts-insurance-claims-restoration-artificial-intelligence-powered-reality)

### Inferences
These are reasoned from the evidence above rather than directly sourced:
- **Staleness:** a twin is a snapshot. Leasing turnover, renovations and damage make it out of date, and recapture needs a person on site. Rising capture cost (technician time) is the binding constraint for large portfolios, which favors a service that flags which spaces need re-scanning and combines cheaper signals (aerial change detection, records such as permits and lease events) with interior scans.
- **Portfolio scale:** enterprise FM and retail buyers manage thousands of sites. Per-space hosting plus per-deliverable add-ons gets expensive, and the twin rarely connects to CAFM/IWMS, lease abstracts or property records. Matterport moved Autodesk Forma integration up to Enterprise, which suggests integration is treated as a premium need.
- **Privacy:** occupied residential interiors (personal items, faces, security layouts) are a known concern for public listing tours and AI ingestion. I found no specific 2025–2026 source; this is unverified.
- Buyers want answers ("how many sq ft of usable office per floor", "what's the roof and HVAC condition", "where is damage") rather than walk-throughs. The AI chat and Property Intelligence moves point the same way.

### Gaps
- No independent surveys of FM, AEC or insurance buyers on capture pain points were found.
- No data was found on capture scheduling lead times, technician (Capture Services) prices, or scan refresh frequency.
- No documented 2025–2026 privacy incidents or complaints specific to Matterport were found.

## 5. Market size and growth drivers

### Takeaway
Third-party market estimates disagree widely, depending on definition, but all point to mid-teens to ~30% CAGR:
- Digital as-builts: about $2.9B (2025) to $6.5B (2030)
- Construction reality-capture automation: about $6.2B (2025) to $16.8B (2034)
- Digital twins for buildings: +$6.4B incremental over 2025–2030 at ~31% CAGR
- Building twin: about $8.9B by 2030

Matterport's own TAM claim (20B spaces, more than $240B, less than 0.1% of the building stock digitized) is a vendor top-down figure, not a market measurement.

### Cited Findings
- Matterport's 10-K: serviceable market of about 1.3B spaces; TAM of more than 4B buildings, 20B spaces and more than $240B; "potential opportunity" over $1T; global building stock about $327T in value with under 0.1% digitized. — [Matterport FY2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819394/000181939425000007/mttr-20241231.htm)
- Digital twin for buildings: +$6.37B increase over 2025–2030 at a 30.7% CAGR (Technavio, May 2026). — [Technavio](https://www.technavio.com/report/digital-twin-for-buildings-market-industry-analysis)

- Digital as-builts solutions: $2.88B (2025) to $6.48B (2030), 17.5% CAGR. — [The Business Research Company](https://www.thebusinessresearchcompany.com/report/digital-as-builts-solutions-market-report)
- Reality capture automation for construction: $6.2B (2025) to $16.8B (2034), ~16.8% CAGR (low-authority publisher). — [Market Intelo](https://marketintelo.com/report/reality-capture-automation-for-construction-market)

### Inferences
- Real spend today is far smaller than the vendor TAM. The largest pure-play (Matterport) earned about $170M in 2024, and construction-capture leaders are private with ARR likely in the low hundreds of millions at most (unverified). Analyst "digital twin" numbers (billions) mostly count BIM/IoT/platform software rather than capture.
- A realistic bottom-up sizing for a CoStar building-intelligence service would start from CoStar's own installed base: Apartments.com's ~250K tours, Matterport's 14M+ spaces, and CoStar's commercial property records. It could charge per building or per space for linked insight rather than per scan.
