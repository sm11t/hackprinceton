## Compare two options

- **Keep office use.** Obtain current suite-specific rent, pass-throughs and occupancy from the broker. Use those figures to set the baseline.
- **Convert to bookable training space.** Obtain a fit-out quote and estimate booked sessions and price per session. Subtract operating costs and the office income forgone. Test delay and fewer bookings before drawing a financial conclusion.

## Insurance and inspection questions

1. What is the existing approved use and occupant load for the exact suite? Ask what egress and sprinkler evidence the inspector needs for the proposed class layout.
2. Does the existing HVAC serve the proposed room and account for the expected headcount? Request equipment records and confirm the footage corresponds to the suite.
3. Which entrance, elevator and parking spaces can visitors use after hours? Ask the broker what the carrier needs to evaluate the proposed public access and operating hours.

The strongest demo finding is the identity check. The same building has a spacious training-room scan and leased-space marketing records, but the app needs an exact suite match before it can use a room measurement and an asking rate in the same scenario.
